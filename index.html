Frequently asked questions

What is UOBCOM 101?

UOBCOM 101 is a student-built workspace for the College of Medicine at the University of Baghdad. It brings your timetable and study life into one calm, organized place.

Who is it for?

It is built for first-year medical students, Year 1 · Term 1, 2026–27. Groups A, B and C are all included.

Who makes UOBCOM 101?

Students, for students. It is an independent project and is not run by the university.
